Why Open-Source AI Matters for This Project

🔒 1. Radical Health & Medical Privacy

Dietary restrictions and allergy histories are sensitive personal health information. Running inference 100% locally via Ollama guarantees that your friend's personal vulnerabilities are never logged by third-party telemetry, ad networks, or cloud surveillance.

💸 2. Zero-Cost Cooking for Everyone

Commercial APIs charge per token. A family or student cooking 3 meals a day should not pay a monthly tax just to eat safely. Local open weights (like Llama 3.2 and Gemma 2) cost exactly $0.00 to run forever.

📶 3. Kitchen-Proof Offline Reliability

Kitchens and basements often have dead zones or spotty Wi-Fi. SafePlate runs on a laptop or local kitchen tablet with zero internet connection required.

🔧 4. Full Custom System Prompts & Hackability

Open models allow us to calibrate strict temperature parameters, fine-tune medical guardrails, and swap models at will without fear of vendor lock-in or sudden deprecation.
